## Target Market

- **Primary**: Middle-to-upper income Kenyan professionals and families
  buying homes in Nairobi (KES 6M – 150M+ range).
- **Secondary**: Diaspora investors (Kenyan expats) buying investment
  property remotely.
- **Tertiary**: Commercial tenants and investors in Upper Hill / Westlands.

## Service Areas (Neighborhoods)

Westlands, Kilimani, Kileleshwa, Lavington, Karen, Muthaiga, Runda,
Loresho, Kitisuru, Langata, Kiambu Road, Riara Road, Upper Hill.

## Property Types

Apartments (1–5BR), DSQ apartments, Villas (4–5BR), Townhouses,
Penthouses, Studios, Commercial space, Land/Plots.

## Brand Identity

### Visual Identity
- **Logo**: Gold-on-dark circular badge with "Delima Realtors" wordmark
  (SVG: `static/lib/assets/logo/delima-logo.svg`).
- **Primary colors**: Gold `#d4af37` / Dark espresso `#1f1810`.
- **Secondary**: Cream `#f8f5f0`, white surfaces, charcoal text `#1f1810`.
- **Typography**: Jost (Google Fonts) — a modern geometric sans.
- **Photography style**: Wide-angle property shots, warm interior lighting,
  lifestyle-context exteriors (poolside, rooftop terrace, city skyline).

### Tone of Voice
- **Professional yet approachable** — expert but not stiff.
- **Data-driven** — "fair market value", "ROI", "appreciation".
- **Trust-first** — "where trust meets excellence" (Facebook tagline).
- **Local expertise** — Nairobi neighborhood-level knowledge.

## Instagram Content Sync Requirements

- **Account**: @delima_realtors (Business/Creator account needed for Graph API).
- **Content**: Images + captions → published as a "Social Feed" gallery on the site.
- **Frequency**: Posts appear every 1–4 weeks (low volume).
- **Sync method**: Instagram Graph API (Meta developer app) — NOT scraping.
- **Fallback**: Manual upload in the admin dashboard (if API access isn't yet configured).
